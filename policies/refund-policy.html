Returns: Unused, unwashed items with original tags can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Sale items and gift cards are not returnable unless faulty.

How to return: Email [hello@yourstore.in] with your order number. We'll arrange a reverse pickup from your address.

Refunds: Once your return passes quality check, the refund is made to your original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 working days. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the item is faulty.

Damaged or wrong item: Email us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund.

Cancellations: Orders can be cancelled before dispatch for a full refund.
